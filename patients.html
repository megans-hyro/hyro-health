Insurance & Billing

Plans we accept

Hyro Health participates with most major insurance plans. Always confirm your specific plan and coverage with your insurer before your visit.

Pay Your Bill Online

Billing basics

  • You'll typically receive a bill within 2–4 weeks after your visit, once your insurance has processed the claim.
  • Interest-free payment plans are available — ask a billing representative or set one up through MyHyro.
  • Uninsured and underinsured patients may qualify for financial assistance based on household income.
  • Request an itemized bill any time by contacting Patient Billing.

Medical Records

Requesting your records

Most patients can view test results, visit summaries, and immunization history directly in MyHyro. For a full copy of your chart, submit a records request.

1

Log in to MyHyroView recent results and visit notes instantly, or start a records request from your account.

2

Or submit a written requestComplete a Release of Information form and return it to your care location or Health Information Management.

3

ProcessingMost requests are fulfilled within 5 business days. Urgent requests (e.g., for continuity of care) are prioritized.

Who can request records

  • The patient (18 or older)
  • A parent or legal guardian of a minor patient
  • An authorized representative with a signed release on file

Pre-Registration

Get ready for your visit

Completing pre-registration ahead of time means less paperwork and a shorter wait when you arrive.

1

Complete forms onlineNew and returning patients can fill out intake, consent, and history forms through MyHyro before their appointment.

2

Gather what you'll needPhoto ID, insurance card, a list of current medications, and any referral or prior imaging your provider requested.

3

Arrive a little earlyNew patients: please arrive 20 minutes before your appointment. Returning patients: 10 minutes is usually enough.

What to bring

  • Government-issued photo ID
  • Insurance card(s)
  • Current medication list
  • Referral or prior records, if applicable

Visiting Policies

Visiting hours & guidelines

Policies can vary slightly by department — please check with your specific care location before you visit.

General visiting hours

  • Most inpatient units: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM daily
  • Immediate family may visit 24/7 in the ICU and Maternity units
  • Outpatient offices follow each location's posted hours

Visitor guidelines

  • Please reschedule your visit if you're feeling unwell
  • A care team member can share any unit-specific visitor limits
  • Every patient has the right to designate support persons and make care decisions with their care team

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Do I need a referral to see a specialist?

Some specialty visits require a referral from your primary care provider, depending on your insurance plan. If you don't have a primary care provider yet, you can find one through Find a Doctor.

Can I request an appointment online?

Yes — many Hyro Health providers offer online booking. Look for the "Book online" badge when searching Find a Doctor, or call the location directly.

Do you offer telehealth visits?

Many providers offer telehealth appointments for certain visit types. You can filter for "Telehealth available" on the Find a Doctor page.

What if I need care after hours?

For medical emergencies, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department. For urgent but non-emergency needs, many primary care locations offer same-day sick visits.

How do I update my contact or insurance information?

You can update your information any time in MyHyro, or let the front desk know at your next visit.

I found a location near me — how do I know what languages are spoken there?

Provider language information is listed on each provider's profile in Find a Doctor, including the languages they speak fluently with patients.
