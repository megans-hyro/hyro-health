Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This is a synthetic demo policy for the Hyro Health fictional health system and does not reflect a real privacy practice.

Overview

Hyro Health ("we," "us," "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of the patients, visitors, and website users we serve. This Privacy Policy describes the information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as when you request an appointment, use our patient portal, or contact us — including your name, contact details, insurance information, and health information relevant to your care. We may also collect limited technical information (such as browser type and pages visited) to help us maintain and improve our website.

How we use your information

  • To schedule and coordinate your care
  • To communicate with you about appointments, billing, and your health information
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, and accreditation requirements
  • To improve our services, website, and patient experience

Protected health information

Health information collected as part of your care is treated as Protected Health Information (PHI) and handled in accordance with applicable healthcare privacy laws. For details on how your health information may be used and disclosed, please request a copy of our Notice of Privacy Practices at any Hyro Health location.

Your choices

You may request access to, and copies of, your medical records at any time — see our Medical Records page for details. You may also opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be directed to our Patient Experience team at (571) 570-5712 or through any Hyro Health location.
